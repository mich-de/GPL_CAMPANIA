GPL Campania shows LPG (autogas) prices for the 5 provinces of Campania, Italy (Avellino, Benevento, Caserta, Napoli, Salerno), read from the Italian Ministry's official price observatory (MIMIT Osservaprezzi carburanti) — the same prices station operators are legally required to report. It plots them on a map and tells you which station is actually the best deal, factoring in distance too.

An Android app that runs entirely on your phone: no server, no account, no API key. The database stays on the device and works offline, showing the last real data saved.

Main features:

* Official prices reported by station operators to the Ministry, with the real reporting date
* OpenStreetMap map via osmdroid, no Google Maps key required
* Pins colored by price tier, derived from the prices actually in the list
* Sort by distance from the device GPS fix or a manually set location
* Favorites that survive updates
* City and brand filters, built dynamically from the data actually present
* Fill-up cost calculator, comparing stations
* Price reporting, if you find a value different from the published one
* Add a missing station, with real geocoding of the address
* Import points of interest from myLPG.eu CSV or KML files
* National LPG averages and regional ranking for Italy
* Fuel news from the Ministry's press room
* Countdown for LPG tank inspection expiry

The guiding principle: the app never invents data. If a coordinate, opening hour, or price is not provided by the source, the field stays empty instead of showing a plausible but fake value.
